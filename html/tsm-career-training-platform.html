KEY CONCEPTS
Readiness Score
A single number rolling up study completion, exam/scenario pass rates, and governance checkpoint adherence across every sector a candidate is assigned. Lives on Readiness Tracker and follows the candidate into Deployment.
HITL (Human-in-the-Loop)
No AI recommendation or candidate milestone advances to execution without a named manager's sign-off. This is what makes the platform's output defensible to enterprise clients and auditors.
Explainability
Every sector answers "why does the AI believe this?" with a confidence score, evidence sources, and reasoning chain — logged and exportable per candidate, per session, per sector.
Cert Tracks vs. Sector Suites
Sector suites (HC, FinOps, Insurance, etc.) teach the day-to-day tools. Paired exam-prep panels (CRCR, AHIP, MLO, OSHA, Paralegal, AB-100) are timed, domain-mapped certification simulations layered on top.
Standalone Tracks
Construction, Legal, and IT Support sit outside the 7 core verticals. They train on real operational war-room tools rather than a formal certification (Construction/Legal have OSHA/NALA cert tracks; IT Support currently proves competence through the tools directly).
Manager vs. Candidate View
Candidates only ever see what's been assigned to them. Managers see everyone — registry, assignment control, pending approvals, and the audit trail behind every unlock.
