⚡ HOW TO USE — HC DENIAL WAR ROOM

STEP 1 — LOAD YOUR DOCUMENT

  • Drop a PDF, DOCX, or image into the intake zone — or paste raw text into the text area.
  • Use a sample (Denial / Prior Auth / Compliance) to demo the system instantly.

STEP 2 — CONFIGURE API KEY

  • Enter your Groq API key in the key field and hit Test. A green dot = connected.
  • Key is saved to localStorage — you only need to do this once per browser.

STEP 3 — FIRE ALL 5 ENGINES

  • E1 · DOCUMENT INTEL — Extracts claim ID, CPT, denial codes, payer, amounts.
  • E2 · ROOT CAUSE — Identifies denial reason, policy violation, coding error.
  • E3 · FINANCIAL IMPACT — Calculates revenue at risk, timely filing exposure.
  • E4 · RECOVERY PLAN — Builds step-by-step appeal plan with deadlines.
  • E5 · RECOVERY NAVIGATOR — Routes next steps to the right staff roles.

STEP 4 — DOWNSTREAM WORKFLOW

  • Engine output feeds into the HC Strategist and Executive Portal via sessionStorage relay.
  • Navigate using the Ecosystem panel to route to other TSM suites.
