How To Use TSM Compliance Command
This is the Compliance Desk — the module that owns exception flags, SoD checks, GAAP adherence, and audit trail across all 11 connected regulatory frameworks. It's one of the modules wired into TSM RCM OS (use the ↩ RCM OS button, top right, to go back, or 📖 RCM OS Guide for the full cross-module walkthrough). Here's how to actually work it.
Quick Start
01
Set your Neural Core API key
Paste your key into the bar at the very top of the page and click SET KEY. Every AI feature on this page — AI Analysis, Ask [Framework] AI, AI Remediation, AI Audit Reports, and the floating AI Assistant — needs this to return live answers instead of an error.
02
Start on Dashboard
Check Priority Alerts first — it's sorted CRITICAL → HIGH → MED → OK. Click ⚡ AI Triage All to have every open alert re-ranked, or click AI FIX → on a single alert to jump straight to a remediation plan for that item.
03
Open the framework tab that matches your issue
HIPAA, SOX / SOC-2, AML / KYC, GDPR, PCI-DSS, NIST / CMMC, ISO 27001, CCPA / State, OIG / CMS, or IRS / Tax — each has its own score gauge, sub-control breakdown, and an Ask [Framework] AI box for questions specific to that regulation.
04
Get a remediation plan
In AI Remediation, describe an issue in your own words (or click ⚡ Generate All Plans to cover every open flag at once) and the AI returns a prioritized, numbered action plan with effort and timeline tags.
05
Track what's due
The right-rail Deadline Tracker and Risk Exposure widgets give you the at-a-glance view; License & Renewals is the full tracker for permits, insurance, and contracts, with a calendar view and one-click renew.
06
Prep for an external audit
Audit & Tax Prep is a dedicated workspace for building the document request list and checklist an auditor or tax preparer will ask for — separate from the day-to-day AI Audit Reports tab, which generates internal compliance reports on demand.
07
Ask anything, anytime
Click the 🤖 bubble in the bottom-right corner any time to open the floating AI Assistant — it stays with you across every tab. Use the Quick Actions grid in the right rail for one-click prompts; the answer opens straight into the assistant.
What's Where
📊 Dashboard
Cross-framework KPIs, Priority Alerts queue, and the Compliance Scorecard — your single starting point every session.
⚖️ Frameworks
HIPAA through IRS/Tax — one tab per regulation, each with its own score, sub-controls, and Ask AI box.
🔖 FinOps Operations
License & Renewals and Audit & Tax Prep — the operational side: tracking expirations and assembling what an auditor will request.
🤖 AI Audit Reports
Generate a full compliance report for any framework, on demand, formatted for CFO or board circulation.
🔧 AI Remediation
Turns a described issue (or every open alert at once) into a prioritized, step-by-step fix plan.
🔌 API Endpoints
Live status and latency for every backend endpoint this page calls — check here first if an AI feature isn't responding.
Honest limitation: the scores, alerts, and endpoint statuses shown throughout this page are demo/blueprint data, not yet a live feed from a compliance data source. Treat every number as illustrative until this module is wired to a real backend.
