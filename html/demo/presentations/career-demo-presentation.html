02 · Candidate Lifecycle

Assign → train → prove readiness → approve → deploy

Course Assignment → Sector Training → HITL → Deployment
One SPA, manager-gated at every stage
Live
STEP 01
Assign & Train
Manager assigns sector courses; candidate works CRCR Study Mode, HC Denial War Room, and scenario drills, with progress checkboxes persisted to localStorage via saveStudyCheck().
STEP 02
Prove Readiness
Readiness Tracker recalculates a live score on every checkbox change via updateReadiness() — confidence bar and placement-status copy update in real time, no page reload.
STEP 03
Approve & Deploy
Manager approves in HITL (approveCandidate()), which immediately unlocks the Earning Path (simulateApproval()) and clears the candidate for Deployment packages.

04 · Status

What's live today

Single-Page App · Client-Side State

The whole candidate journey runs inside one SPA (tsm-career-training-platform.html), panel-switched by switchTo() — no separate page loads, no relay chain like the operational war rooms. What's real: study-checklist persistence to localStorage (survives a refresh), the Readiness Tracker's live score calculation, and HITL's approve/revise state changes that immediately unlock the Earning Path in the same session.

What's illustrative, not wired: the Course Assignment checkboxes (mockup of Sarah Kim's enrollment, don't live-filter another candidate's nav), the HITL approve/revise decision (changes the DOM for this session, isn't written to a backend yet), and the Deployment Center's packages (static content for the demo candidate, not generated on the fly).
