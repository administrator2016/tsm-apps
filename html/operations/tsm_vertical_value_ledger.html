Methodology & honesty notes

The dollar figures below are placeholder monthly-recoverable-value estimates, not measured client data — TSM has no live financial-recovery run-rate yet across most verticals. Replace them with real numbers as HonorHealth, First American, and Ameris (and future pilots) generate actual recovery figures. Commission rates and client-mix weights are the two levers that drive every number here; both live in the companion workbook's Assumptions tab, not hardcoded per row.

Feature claims are drawn from MASTER_VERTICAL_WALKTHROUGH.md — a document built by confirming onClick bindings, relay writes, and export functions directly against the live HTML/JS, not by naming convention. Market-incumbent comparisons are category-level, not sourced from current published pricing — verify competitor pricing before using these in a client-facing deck.
